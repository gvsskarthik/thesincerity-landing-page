Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This notice describes information submitted through the public website of Sincerity (OPC) Private Limited. Individual products may have separate privacy information.

Information you provide

When you use an enquiry form, you provide your name, email address, message, and, optionally, your company or profile link. We also receive the product, service, support, hiring, or career topic associated with your enquiry. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information.

How information is used

Information submitted in an enquiry is used to review and respond to that enquiry, discuss a product or project, provide support, or communicate about career opportunities, as applicable.

Website and form providers

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, loads fonts from Google Fonts, and sends enquiry forms through FormSubmit to support@thesincerity.in. These providers receive information needed to provide their services, which can include technical connection information. FormSubmit receives the information you submit in a form. Their processing is subject to their own privacy practices.

Browser storage

The current website application does not store enquiry details in your browser’s local storage or include advertising or analytics scripts. Information you enter remains in the open form while you use it; closing the form clears those fields. Hosting and third-party providers may process request information separately.

Privacy questions and requests

For questions about this notice or to request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, email support@thesincerity.in. Describe your request and the enquiry it relates to so we can identify the relevant information.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the website or its information handling changes. The date above identifies the current version.
