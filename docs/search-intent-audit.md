# Search intent audit and content expansion

Reviewed 23 October 2026 for `https://thesincerity.in/`.

## What was missing

The earlier implementation established useful technical foundations: twelve prerendered routes, page-specific metadata, canonical links, a sitemap, readable HTML, and structured company/product/service information. However, OptiMediCare's content described a generic laboratory workflow and did not clearly name several relevant audiences and use cases. The user correctly identified the missing medical laboratory, pathology lab, blood test report, and health checkup package terminology.

The service pages similarly explained the general work but omitted some normal commercial terminology: website design, landing pages, mobile app development, SaaS MVP development, API/webhook integration, application maintenance, and WhatsApp Business API integration. More specific wording helps people recognise whether a page answers their needs. Metadata alone does not replace a useful visible explanation.

## Research method and limits

This review compares the marketing site's content with its existing product review and the available application source, then groups relevant queries by the job a prospective client needs to do. It is not a paid keyword-tool export or evidence of measured search volumes. Search Console account data, query impressions, competitors' traffic, and keyword difficulty were not available; no volume or ranking figures are invented.

Google's [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) advises considering the language different users use, and says its language matching can understand related queries without every exact wording appearing. The guide also states that Google does not use the keywords meta tag. [Google's spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#keyword-stuffing) prohibit unnatural repetition and lists created to manipulate rankings. Its [helpful content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) favours substantial explanations made for the intended audience.

Accordingly, this implementation expands the relevant subject matter on real pages. It does not add one thousand phrases to each page, hide text, create near-identical pages for synonyms, or turn a product page into a list of test names. A thousand possible searches can represent a much smaller set of useful questions.

These are coverage examples, not promises to rank or a requirement that every phrase appear verbatim. Natural variants such as lab/laboratory and centre/center can relate to the same underlying intent. Deliberate misspellings such as “blood teast remort” are not added to the site's professional copy.

### Catalogue discipline terminology

Read-only evidence: `/srv/ssdc/ssdc-frontend-react/src/home/sub-tasks/test/new-tests.tsx` and `new-group.tsx` each define these categories: Hematology, Biochemistry, Serology, Microbiology, Clinical Pathology (Urine), Endocrinology (Thyroid/Hormones), and Immunology. They also define configurable parameters, units, normal-value text/ranges, and test details. The new visible catalogue explanation reflects those actual categories, using haematology/hematology to make the spelling clear for an international audience.

[NABL's scope reference](https://nablmelt.qci.org.in/Laboratory/new-scheme/scope-applicable.php) independently confirms standard discipline terminology and examples such as CBC/haemogram, biochemical tests, and clinical pathology. It is used only to check language. It does not establish that Sincerity or OptiMediCare is NABL accredited, or that any named test comes preinstalled or clinically validated.

Test names such as CBC, haemoglobin, blood glucose, HbA1c, lipid profile, liver function tests, and kidney function tests are legitimate laboratory vocabulary. They should be used in a future product example only after the relevant catalogue configuration, report format, and workflow have been reviewed. Listing hundreds of medical tests would not establish their support and could attract people looking to book tests rather than buy software.

The [CDC's LIMS introduction](https://reach.cdc.gov/sites/default/files/video-transcripts/What%20is%20a%20LIMS_508.pdf) explains that LIS is used in clinical contexts and that broad LIMS products can include specimen tracking, instrument connections, inventory, quality processes, and other capabilities. Those terms are not a licence to imply that every such capability exists in OptiMediCare. This page retains the more precise lab management software description.

### Important distinction

The page offers software to laboratories. It does not book blood tests, perform human blood testing, provide health checkups, interpret a person's blood report, or supply medical advice. A visible FAQ makes that distinction. User interest in “blood test” is addressed through blood test **report management software**, not by pretending Sincerity is a testing laboratory.

## Topics deliberately deferred

Do not add the following as delivered or certified features without evidence:

- Clinical diagnosis, automatic blood report interpretation, treatment recommendations, AI diagnosis, or clinical accuracy guarantees.
- NABL/ISO accreditation, HIPAA/GDPR compliance, government approval, or an accreditation-ready guarantee.
- Analyser interfaces, barcode specimen tracking, sample transport, quality-control modules, reagent inventory, a full LIMS/LIS feature set, HL7/FHIR interoperability, or EHR integration that has not been verified.
- Histopathology, cytopathology, genetic testing, radiology/PACS, blood bank workflows, or a patient booking portal as existing OptiMediCare modules.
- Official Meta partner/BSP status, unrestricted or free WhatsApp API usage, guaranteed message delivery, bulk unsolicited marketing, or unsupported broadcast/CRM features.
- Particular Android/iOS implementation frameworks, guaranteed store publishing, cloud-vendor certifications, round-the-clock support, or fixed response times without a verified service commitment.
- AI/ML development as a fully described specialist service, SEO/marketing retainers, e-commerce/payment platforms, hardware, chips, electronics, or other services not established by the current offerings. The business can consider new service pages when scope and evidence are supplied.
- Fake international offices, customer logos, testimonials, invented case-study outcomes, or claims that keywords can guarantee number-one rankings.

## What changed in this implementation

- Updated OptiMediCare's concise search title and description to identify medical/pathology software and the verified reporting/package use cases.
- Added visible use-case explanations for medical/pathology laboratories, blood test report management, health checkup packages, and the existing catalogue disciplines.
- Added product FAQs for the intended audience, report/package configuration, and software-versus-testing distinction.
- Updated WACentral's product wording and metadata to clearly identify WhatsApp Business API/Meta Cloud API integration and chatbot routing.
- Refined each of the six service pages' visible introductions, relevant scope descriptions, titles/descriptions where useful, and practical FAQs.
- Preserved twelve indexable routes. No thin synonym pages, hidden keyword text, bulk keyword meta tags, fabricated reviews, or unsupported product features were introduced.

Google may build search snippets from visible content rather than a supplied description, and may rewrite title links. The changes follow its [snippet guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet) and [title-link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) by keeping descriptions page-specific and titles concise. Neither guarantees a particular display.

## Measurement after publication

Search Console verification and sitemap submission remain owner-account actions unless an authenticated connection or verification value becomes available. Once query data is available, group impressions and clicks by these intents, check the destination pages, and improve explanations that users actually need. Add distinct guides or genuine customer-approved examples when they offer substantial new value. Rank position, indexing, rich results, and timing remain Google's decisions; the code does not prove any of those outcomes.

No source or build files in `/srv/ssdc` or `/srv/wa-central` were edited for this audit.
